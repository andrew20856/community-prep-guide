Bagyo at baha

What to do, when it matters

Keep these steps simple. Local evacuation orders always come first.

01 / BEFORE

Get ready early

  • Follow PAGASA forecasts and official barangay or LGU advisories.
  • Agree on a meeting place and how to contact each other.
  • Move valuables and documents above expected flood level.
  • Charge phones, lights, and power banks; refill safe water containers.
02 / WHEN TOLD TO EVACUATE

Leave while routes are safe

  • Go to your designated evacuation center or higher ground.
  • Bring your go-bag, medicines, documents, and essential mobility aids.
  • Bring children, older adults, and pets; do not wait for floodwater.
  • Switch off electricity only if it is safe and you have been instructed.
03 / DURING FLOODING

Stay out of floodwater

  • Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater.
  • Keep away from downed wires, drains, and fast-moving water.
  • If trapped, move to higher ground and call for help.
  • Drink safe or boiled water; follow local health guidance.
